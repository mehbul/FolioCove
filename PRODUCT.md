# FolioCove

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Browser PDF utilities. The owner confirmed that visitors should choose a tool and process a file immediately, rather than visit a separate marketing homepage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Ten core routes and a wider catalog of 88 tools. Documents are processed on the device. There are no document-upload endpoints or remote analytics. English OCR may download a language model. Keep existing tool behavior, IDs, downloads, error states, accessibility and local preferences. Full sanitization is disabled and redaction is experimental. The current site is owner-private; public operator details remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

FolioCove. The owner requested a premium website inspired by real design such as Airbnb, using design-taste-frontend, impeccable and emil-design-eng. This is inspiration, not an Airbnb affiliation or use of its proprietary assets.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing functional code, ten tool routes, generated PDF checks and browser workflow tests. No customer testimonials or commercial usage numbers have been supplied.

Local expansion: AES-256 protection/unlocking, lossless qpdf compression and recovery, JPG ZIP, native text DOCX/XLSX, image-slide PPTX, form authoring, drawn ink, image watermarking, shapes, side-by-side comparison, and browser-dependent AI summaries. No validated PDF/A or remote signature workflow. Office layout fidelity, auto form detection, URL fetching and layout-preserving translation remain unavailable.
