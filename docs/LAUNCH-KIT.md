# FolioCove public beta launch kit

## Short launch post

I built FolioCove, a free, open-source PDF workspace that processes selected documents in your browser. No sign-up or document-upload endpoint.

Try merging two PDFs, extracting pages, or turning images into a PDF: https://mehbul.github.io/FolioCove/

It is a public beta. Keep originals and inspect outputs. AI tools depend on browser support, redaction is experimental, and verified sanitization/PDF/A are unavailable.

Source: https://github.com/mehbul/FolioCove
Feedback: https://github.com/mehbul/FolioCove/issues — synthetic examples only; do not attach personal documents.

If you try one task, tell me: Did you finish it? What confused you? Would you use it again?
